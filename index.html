Phase 6

Ion Recombination & Polarity Corrections

Separate correction-factor calculations with optional transfer to the TG-51 worksheet.

Pion — Ion Recombination Correction

Two-voltage method for applicable pulsed radiation conditions. Enter voltage magnitudes and corresponding positive-magnitude readings.

Calculated Pion —

Formula — applicable pulsed-beam approximation

Pion = (1 − VH/VL) / (MH/ML − VH/VL)

This simplified model does not implement continuous-beam or pulsed-scanned alternatives. Confirm protocol applicability.

Ppol — Polarity Correction

Enter signed readings and select the reference measurement polarity.

Calculated Ppol —

Formula

Ppol = (|M+| + |M−|) / (2 × |Mref|)

Measurements must be made under equivalent conditions. Verify electrometer polarity and leakage conventions.
