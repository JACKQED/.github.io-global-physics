Phase 6 · New Tools

Ion Recombination & Polarity Corrections

Separate correction-factor calculations with optional transfer to the TG-51 worksheet.

Pion — Ion Recombination Correction

Two-voltage method for applicable pulsed radiation conditions. Enter the magnitude of each bias voltage and the corresponding positive-magnitude chamber reading.

Calculated Pion —

Formula — applicable pulsed-beam approximation

Pion = (1 − VH/VL) / (MH/ML − VH/VL)

VH and VL are voltage magnitudes. The calculation assumes appropriate two-voltage measurements under stable conditions and a supported pulsed-radiation regime. It does not implement the continuous-beam or pulsed-scanned alternatives.

Ppol — Polarity Correction

Enter readings with their measured signs. Choose the polarity used for the reference dosimetry measurement.

Calculated Ppol —

Formula

Ppol = (|M+| + |M−|) / (2 × |Mref|)

Mref is the reading at the chosen reference polarity. Readings should be acquired under equivalent measurement conditions, with appropriate handling of electrometer polarity and leakage.

Clinical limitations: The Phase 6 calculators provide arithmetic assistance only. The two-voltage Pion approximation is not universally applicable. Verify chamber type, beam delivery mode, voltage ratio, collection efficiency, measurement conditions, electrometer conventions and the applicable TG-51 protocol or addendum. Results are not clinically validated and must not be used alone for treatment beam calibration.
